## The pattern holds

Still looking for:
- Systems with internal logic
- Immediate feedback
- Tactile or tangible
- Low maintenance floor
- Cheap entry
- Pairs with existing interests

The freshest intersections: **36 (information theory)** feeds directly into your daily work but from the math side. **34 (game theory)** is civic tech rocket fuel. **37 (bouldering)** is the physical hobby most correlated with programmer brains. **48 (CTF)** is your infra stack turned into sport.
