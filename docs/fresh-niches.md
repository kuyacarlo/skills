## Pattern

The niches that fit you share these traits:
- **Systems with internal logic** (not random, not purely aesthetic)
- **Immediate or frequent feedback** (not "wait 6 months for results")
- **Tactile or tangible output** (not purely abstract)
- **Solo-friendly but community exists** (not team-dependent)
- **Low maintenance floor, high ceiling** (can do 5 min or 5 hours)
- **Cheap entry, depth over time** (not pay-to-play)
- **Pairs with existing interests** (doesn't require a new identity)
