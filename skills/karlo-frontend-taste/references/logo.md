# Logo

Rule 18 in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) is the ban. This note is how to design the mark.

## Taste

Stated across one iteration, from a rejected cartoon face to the mark he kept.

- Minimal, sleek, and corporate, with some personality.
- Flat. One color. One path when that can carry the shape.
- Personality comes from the silhouette and from cut-outs, such as a mask or a nose pad.
- No eyes. He rejected the variants that had them.
- No cartoon expression.
- Rounded terminals. A sharp V chin read as awkward.
- Check the silhouette. Round ears can read as a bear. Pointed ears can read as a cat.

## Sheets

This is the process that worked. Do not keep nudging one rejected idea.

1. After a rejection, make one sheet with 3 or 4 directions that are actually different.
2. Show each direction large, at 24px inside a wordmark lockup, and in one color on a light ground.
3. Wait for the pick.
4. Make a second sheet with 2 or 3 small variants of that pick.
5. Stop when he picks. Do not add eyes back in a later pass.
