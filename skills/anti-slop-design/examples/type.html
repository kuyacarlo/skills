Fonts, sizes, and spacing that read as generated.

Italic serif display headline

Fix: a workhorse sans that fits the product.

Ultra-thin weight, crushed tracking

Fix: regular or semibold weight, default tracking.

All-caps body with wide tracking

Fix: sentence case. Uppercase only on short labels.

Tiny grey text

Fix: body at 1.5rem or larger. Muted text still meets 4.5:1.

Stub last line

Fix: text-wrap: balance on headings, pretty on paragraphs.

Lines too long

Fix: about 65–75 characters per line.

Justified text

Fix: start-aligned body text. No rivers of space.

Flat hierarchy

Fix: vary size, weight, and space before adding a second font.
