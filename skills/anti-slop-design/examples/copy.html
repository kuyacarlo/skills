Words that pitch instead of inform.

Slogan triplet "Not X, Y" Stacked conditions Invented social proof Explained design Competitor trademark Button copy Repeated text

Slogan triplet and superlatives

Fix: one sentence that says what it does.

"Not X, Y" with an em dash

Fix: state the point. Full stops over dashes.

Stacked-condition subhead

Fix: say how it is priced, in one plain sentence.

Invented social proof

Fix: no fake logos, counts, or quotes. Unknowns are placeholders. No list, no row.

Copy that explains the design

Fix: delete the narration. The links and the order already show it.

Competitor trademark in the tagline

Fix: name the category. Competitors only in a neutral comparison table.

Button copy

Fix: a verb plus an object. The label says what you get.

Same text repeated in one container

Fix: say it once, where it matters.
